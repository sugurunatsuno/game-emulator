# Mactician Release Notes — 1.2.3

- Improve performance diagnostics to explain why a sample has no recognized
  gameplay context or why a frame measurement failed.
- Collect bounded counts for capture and recognition outcomes, scene transitions,
  requested game language, screenshot-size categories, and collection timings.
  Screenshots and recognized text remain on your Mac and are never uploaded.
- Preserve the existing gameplay classification rules and sampling cadence while
  collecting evidence for targeted recognition fixes.

Build 52 for Apple Silicon, macOS 12 or later.
